## Banner suitability

The store does not expose an existing wide photographic scene of its products. Use actual product packshots as code-native layered images over a locally designed banner background. BIOHYALUX brightening is 1800×1200; the GENOSYS, M.A.D and FUSION packshots are 900×600. The old site-background assets are abstract green wave patterns, not product photography. Homecare/facial portrait images are illustrations, not real product images.

The HairCOCKTAILPlus source image belongs to TOSKANI; it must not be labeled FUSION.
