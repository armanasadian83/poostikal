Vazirmatn variable font

Vazirmatn-Arabic.woff2 is vazirmatn-arabic-wght-normal.woff2.
Vazirmatn-Latin.woff2 is vazirmatn-latin-wght-normal.woff2.
Both files support normal style and weights 100 through 900.
Files are bundled locally; no external font service is required at runtime.
